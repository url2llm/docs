Open [Settings > Billing](https://url2llm.com/dashboard/settings/billing) for the active organization. Members can view billing; organization owners and admins can change subscriptions, buy credits, and configure auto-recharge.

## Choose a plan

| Plan | Monthly price (USD) | Included credits | Prepaid price per 1,000 credits (USD) |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Free | $0 | 1,000 | $3.00 |
| Hobby | $19 | 8,000 | $3.00 |
| Growth | $49 | 25,000 | $2.50 |
| Pro | $99 | 120,000 | $2.00 |
| Scale | $299 | 400,000 | $1.50 |

The current dashboard and [pricing page](https://url2llm.com/#pricing) show available purchase options. Listed prices are before applicable tax. A plan is an organization allowance, shared by all its keys.

Monthly credits are used before purchased credits. Unused monthly credits expire at the end of their period. The Free allowance renews monthly; paid-plan renewals add the next allowance after payment is confirmed. Purchased credits carry forward and do not expire. The renewal date shown in Billing is separate from the calendar-month auto-recharge cap.

## Buy prepaid credits

Buy additional credits through [Billing](https://url2llm.com/dashboard/settings/billing). Enter $5 to $1,000, review the credits at your plan's prepaid rate, and complete checkout. Credits are added after payment confirmation; returning from checkout alone does not confirm payment.

Resolve a pending or uncertain purchase before starting another payment. Failed purchases add no credits. Refunds remove the associated credits; if already spent, those credits can become debt covered by future grants. Contact [support](mailto:support@url2llm.com) about refunds.

## Enable auto-recharge

Auto-recharge is optional. In Billing, set a credit threshold, USD purchase amount, and monthly USD cap. A recharge must add more credits than the threshold, and the cap must cover at least one recharge. Authorize a saved payment method when prompted; this $0 authorization adds no credits.

For example, on Hobby, $10 buys 3,333 credits before tax. With a threshold of 1,000 credits and a $30 cap, at most three such automatic purchases can be initiated in a UTC calendar month. Credit quantities are rounded down at the current plan rate.

The cap includes pending and initiated automatic purchases before tax; it excludes manual purchases and subscriptions. An unresolved earlier payment prevents another automatic charge. Failed payments pause recharge. Disabling recharge prevents new charges but does not cancel a payment already submitted.

## Manage your subscription

Monthly plans, renewals, invoices, and cancellation are managed through [Billing](https://url2llm.com/dashboard/settings/billing). Check the current plan and period end after payment confirmation. A confirmed renewal grants the next monthly allowance; purchased credits remain separate.

## Understand available and reserved credits

Billing separates available credits from credits reserved for work in progress. A request reserves its price before execution, then charges or releases that reservation according to the result. Multiple keys can consume the same balance concurrently.

An insufficient balance returns `402`. A quote checks price without charging, but a later request still needs enough available credits. Read [quote and credits](/guides/quote) for operation prices and [usage and activity](/account/usage) for individual charges.
